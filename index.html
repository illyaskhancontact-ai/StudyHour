Privacy Policy

StudyHour · Effective date: October 4, 2026

In short

StudyHour works fully offline. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no user accounts. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the StudyHour Android app (package name: com.illayasjamdev.studyhour) handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. Data stored on your device

To work properly, StudyHour saves the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • The subjects you create
  • Your completed study sessions (subject, time and duration)
  • Your settings, such as theme, focus duration, break duration and daily goal

This data is never sent to us or to any third party.

4. Permissions and internet access

StudyHour does not request any special permissions and does not request internet access. The app cannot send your data over the network.

5. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not use any third-party SDKs that collect data.

6. Device backup

Android may include app data in your device backup if you have turned on backup for your Google account or device. This is controlled by your device and Google settings, not by StudyHour. You can manage or turn it off in your phone's system settings.

7. Deleting your data

You are in control of your data. You can:

  • Open Settings → Clear history to delete all study sessions and progress
  • Delete individual subjects from the Subjects screen
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device

8. Children's privacy

StudyHour does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or shared, the app is suitable for students of all ages.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Please check it from time to time.
